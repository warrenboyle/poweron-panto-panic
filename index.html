PANTOPANIC!

Drive an electric bus. Pick up riders. Stay on time. Don't run flat.

From PowerON, Canada's end-to-end fleet electrification partner.

Made by the intern

Pick your transit agency

How to drive (about 40 seconds):
  1. Hold GO (or Space / →) to drive. Let go to brake.
  2. Stop with the whole bus inside the yellow box to pick up riders. Hit the schedule time for an on-time bonus. Early costs a little, and driving past riders costs a lot.
  3. Faster driving drains the battery faster. Stop in the green box under the charger and hold CHARGE (or ↓ / C) to lower the pantograph.
  4. Reach the terminal for a charge bonus and a time bonus. Run out of battery and your run is over.

Agency names are used for fun. This game is not affiliated with any transit agency.
